Leash

A spending firewall for AI agents. Every spend the agent tried, allowed or blocked, read live from the contract.

What you are looking at

An AI agent on NEAR testnet holds only a function-call key that can call one method, request_spend, on the Leash contract. A poisoned coffee shop page told it to send its whole balance to attacker-vault.testnet. The contract checked the owner's policy and said no. Every attempt, allowed or blocked, is in the log below with the rule that decided it.

Rows to look for: the 0.05 NEAR latte Allowed; the 8.36 NEAR drain Blocked / MerchantNotAllowlisted; the 0.6 NEAR inflated receipt Blocked / PerTxCap; a 0.3 NEAR spend Approved after owner co-sign; an injected memo to the allowlisted merchant Blocked / EngineRisk (signed engine score 60, limit 30); a replayed verdict Blocked / BadAttestation.

Code and reproducible steps: github.com/apexweb-adam/leash. Contract on the explorer: leash-11020911.testnet.
